About the shared task

Writing in an author's style

AbjadStyleTransfer explores literary authorship style transfer. Given an input passage and a target author, participating systems generate a rewritten passage that reflects the target author's stylistic characteristics while retaining the source meaning.

Input

Source passage and target author

A passage, typically in a neutral or formal style, together with the identity or style of a target literary author.

Output

Style-transferred passage

A generated Urdu passage that captures the target author's writing style without materially changing the original content.

Research challenge

Stylistic fidelity

Capture distinctive literary voice, lexical preferences and expression rather than merely paraphrasing the input.

Research challenge

Meaning preservation

Balance stylistic transformation with semantic faithfulness and natural, fluent Urdu.
